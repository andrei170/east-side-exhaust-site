Common Questions

Good to know before you come in.

What does the 1-year labor warranty cover?

Every service we perform in our bays is backed by a full year of labor coverage. If a repair we did needs a return visit inside that year, the labor is on us. It applies to every line, from transmission rebuilds to brake jobs.

What does ATSG-certified mean?

ATSG is the Automatic Transmission Service Group, the technical body that trains and certifies transmission specialists. The certification requires ongoing training on new transmission generations as manufacturers release them. Almost no general repair shop in the Green Bay metro carries it. We do.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. We offer free same-day estimates on most jobs before any repair order is opened. Call us with the symptom, the vehicle, and the year, and we will tell you whether it is a same-day estimate or a job that needs the vehicle in the bay first.

What kinds of vehicles do you work on?

Cars, trucks, and SUVs across most common makes and models, late-model and older. As a 52-year shop, we have seen a wide range of vehicles and transmission types come through the bays.

Do I need an appointment?

For most work, calling ahead is the best way to line up a drop-off time and confirm same-day availability. Call (920) 536-4243 to set it up.
